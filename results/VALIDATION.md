**Solar yield:** Radiocity is 4-7% *below* PVGIS in every city, so it does not overstate solar energy. Most of the gap is the weather data itself: Radiocity uses NASA POWER data for the single year 2024, which was 6-10% less sunny than PVGIS's 2005-2023 satellite average. Per unit of sunlight, the two conversion models agree to within about 1-3%.
