Generated by `python scripts/run_experiments.py` from real 2024 hourly NASA POWER climate data for four Nigerian cities, using the standard physics documented in docs/MODEL.md. Independent checks against exact physical laws, the European Commission's PVGIS solar model and published measurements are in VALIDATION.md (`python scripts/validate.py`).
