# Radiocity Vision

*This document is the source of truth for the project's direction. Every module, experiment and claim should serve it. Anything that does not is removed - unless it is the only route to something the vision needs.*

## The original intention

> Create a system where future objects, machines, cars and equipment are powered by invisible energy - radiation, radio waves, any invisible energy - instead of oil, conventional panels, batteries that need plugging in, and other familiar sources.
>
> A car, for instance, would carry a receiver - integrated, coated or painted on - so it can charge and recharge at any time. It must store energy, regulate itself automatically when full, and save energy for later. Regulating and managing the energy is as important as capturing it. The same applies to smaller and bigger machinery and equipment - anything possible.
>
> The aim is to build a company: seek funding, collaborate, and publish.

— Muhammad Ibrahim Gimba

## The vision, stated precisely

**Radiocity is energy autonomy from invisible energy.**

Every device - from a sensor to a phone to a car to a factory machine - carries receivers (solar skins and coatings, antennas, heat converters, wireless-power coils) that capture whatever invisible energy is available around it or sent to it. An intelligent power system **stores** that energy, **regulates** it (charge control, stop-when-full, protection) and **spends it wisely** (critical needs first, save for later, use surplus when it is free). The goal is to remove plugs, fuel and battery swaps wherever physics allows.

## Two kinds of invisible energy

| | Around us (ambient) | From processes (heat) | Sent to the device (beamed) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Examples | sunlight, radio waves (Wi-Fi, cellular, broadcast) | furnace, engine and exhaust heat | wireless-charging pads, electrified road lanes, microwave and laser power beams |
| Strength | sunlight strong; radio waves extremely weak | strong where hot processes exist | as strong as the transmitter |
| Cost | free, needs only receivers | free, needs a hot source nearby | needs transmitters (infrastructure) |

The central insight from Radiocity's own results: **ambient energy alone can fully power small devices, partly power vehicles, and cannot power heavy machinery - beamed energy closes the gap.** A complete invisible-energy system therefore combines all three, managed by one intelligent power system. See [PHYSICAL_LIMITS.md](PHYSICAL_LIMITS.md) and [../results/RESULTS.md](../results/RESULTS.md).

## What Radiocity is building

1. **The Radiocity engine (this repository)** - a physics-based, data-driven simulator and design tool that answers, for any device in any place: *how much of its energy can invisible energy supply, with which receivers, how much storage, and which management policy?*
2. **The Radiocity power manager** - the "brain": harvest from any mix of receivers, store, regulate, forecast and allocate. This is designed to become device firmware and is the core of the company.
3. **Receivers and infrastructure partnerships** - solar skins and coatings, rectennas, heat-to-power converters, wireless-power transmitters - integrated per product.

## Publishing and protection

Publishing an idea before filing a patent can destroy its patentability in most countries. File first (for example a provisional or local application) for anything novel - especially the power-management method - then publish. Radiocity's documents and results are written so they can become a paper once protection is in place.

## Scope rules

- **In scope:** any energy form that reaches a device without a wire or fuel - sunlight, radio waves, heat radiation, waste heat, magnetic/microwave/laser power transfer - plus storage, regulation, forecasting and allocation.
- **Never in scope:** claims that break conservation of energy or the second law of thermodynamics (perpetual motion, "free energy" from equilibrium surroundings).
- **Future research is welcome:** anything currently listed as a limitation is a research target, not a boundary - unless a physical law forbids it.
