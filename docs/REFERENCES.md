# References

## Purpose

This is the working master bibliography for Portfolio A: **Adaptive Multi-Spectral Radiative Energy Harvesting**.

References are added as the repository develops. Literature-derived equations, assumptions, parameter ranges, benchmarks, and physical claims should be traceable to an entry here where practical.

## Citation Policy

- Prefer **peer-reviewed journal articles, conference papers, standards, textbooks, and authoritative institutional datasets** over blogs or general websites.
- When the future paper is prepared, references will be formatted consistently in **APA style** unless the target journal requires another style.
- DOI and other persistent identifiers should be recorded whenever available.
- Web sources may be used for authoritative datasets, standards, institutional technical documentation, or current specifications, but should not replace scholarly sources when a suitable scholarly source exists.
- Repository-specific assumptions and original calculations should be clearly distinguished from literature-derived values.

## Core References

### Solar conversion and thermodynamic limits

1. Shockley, W., & Queisser, H. J. (1961). Detailed balance limit of efficiency of p-n junction solar cells. *Journal of Applied Physics, 32*(3), 510–519. https://doi.org/10.1063/1.1736034

   **Use in project:** Fundamental detailed-balance limit for single-junction photovoltaic conversion and the distinction between incident solar energy and realistically convertible electrical energy.

2. Rühle, S. (2016). Tabulated values of the Shockley–Queisser limit for single junction solar cells. *Solar Energy, 130*, 139–147. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2016.02.015

   **Use in project:** Reference values for single-junction photovoltaic efficiency limits under standard solar illumination conditions.

### Radio-frequency energy harvesting

3. Wagih, M., Weddell, A. S., & Beeby, S. (2020). Rectennas for radio-frequency energy harvesting and wireless power transfer: A review of antenna design. *IEEE Antennas and Propagation Magazine, 62*(5), 95–107. https://doi.org/10.1109/MAP.2020.3012872

   **Use in project:** RF energy harvesting architecture, rectenna design constraints, antenna performance, and ambient RF harvesting limitations.

4. Kaur, N., Sharma, N., & Kumar, N. (2018). RF energy harvesting and storage system of rectenna: A review. *Indian Journal of Science and Technology, 11*(25), 1–5. https://doi.org/10.17485/ijst/2018/v11i25/114309

   **Use in project:** Background on RF harvesting, rectennas, rectification, and energy storage.

## Authoritative Technical Sources

5. National Renewable Energy Laboratory (NREL). Standard solar photovoltaic test-condition documentation.
